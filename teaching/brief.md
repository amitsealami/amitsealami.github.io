Here are ideas across a few dimensions:

Testimonials section

Display quotes in a card grid (2 columns) rather than a stacked list — lets the eye scan without feeling like a wall of text
Pull one or two hero quotes out large (bigger font, maybe a subtle highlight color) above the rest, to reward skimmers
Add a small attribution detail under each quote — course name, year — gives them more context and credibility
Subtle quotation mark graphic as a visual anchor per card

Philosophy section

The Why / What / How trio is begging to be a 3-column visual or a simple icon row — right now it reads as bullets, but it has the structure of a framework worth displaying as one
Consider a two-panel layout — Relatability on the left, Critical Thinking on the right — rather than stacking them vertically
A thin divider or accent color strip to make this section feel distinct from the testimonials above it

Courses section

Distinguish current vs. past more visually — a "Now" badge or colored dot next to the current course pops immediately vs. a plain header
Even a minimal course card (title, semester, a one-line description) beats a bare list

Page-wide

The page likely has very little color — one accent color used consistently (section labels, the framework trio, course badge) would create hierarchy without redesigning anything
Typographic scale — if the quote text and body text are the same size, bumping quotes up slightly (and maybe italicizing) visually separates them from prose
A subtle background alternation between sections (white → very light gray → white) is a low-effort way to signal "new section" without hard dividers
